## Paper information

- **Title:** 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering
- **Authors:** Bernhard Kerbl, Georgios Kopanas, Thomas Leimkühler, and George Drettakis
- **Publication:** SIGGRAPH 2023 / ACM Transactions on Graphics

- [Read on arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04079)
- [Official GitHub repository](https://github.com/graphdeco-inria/gaussian-splatting)
- [Official project page](https://repo-sam.inria.fr/fungraph/3d-gaussian-splatting/)

## Paper overview

The paper introduces a method for reconstructing and rendering scenes from photographs using 3D Gaussians. It combines an explicit scene representation, optimization with adaptive density control, and a visibility-aware splatting renderer to support high-quality, real-time novel-view synthesis.

This overview summarizes the original authors' work. The method and implementation are credited to the authors linked above.
